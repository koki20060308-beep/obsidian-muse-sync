Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

About this application

Obsidian Muse Sync is a personal-use utility operated by its owner. It copies Markdown files from the owner's Obsidian vault to the owner's Google Drive using rclone.

Data accessed

The application requests the Google Drive drive.file permission to create, access, and update files used by the application.

How data is used

Google Drive access is used solely to synchronize the owner's Markdown notes to a designated Google Drive folder.

Data storage and sharing

Files are stored in the owner's Google Drive account. OAuth credentials are stored locally by rclone. The operator does not sell or intentionally share Google user data with third parties.

Data deletion

The synchronization process does not automatically delete files from Google Drive. The owner can manually delete synchronized files and revoke OAuth access through Google Account settings.

Contact

This application is intended exclusively for personal use.
